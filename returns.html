Returns & cancellations

Check your design before ordering. Let us know if anything is wrong.

Updated 9 October 2026 · Applies to orders placed after this policy is published

ShippingReturns & cancellationsPrivacy policyTerms of serviceContact us
Before production starts, you can request cancellation and a full refund. Once production starts, we do not accept change-of-mind returns. Faults, transit damage, incorrect items or items that differ from your confirmed design remain eligible for a free remake, replacement or refund.

1. Cancelling or changing a design

To cancel or change an order, email us promptly with your order number. We will check whether production has started. Before production starts, cancellation receives a full refund. Once production starts, change-of-mind cancellations or changes usually cannot be accepted. If a change is still possible, we will explain the options and any cost and obtain your agreement first.

Before paying, check the pet’s name, portrait, colours, charms and the owner’s name and phone number. If we make something that does not match your confirmed details, we will correct it free of charge. If you entered incorrect details, contact us promptly and we will help according to production progress.

2. Faulty, damaged, incorrect or missing items

Please contact us, preferably within 7 days of receipt, with your order number, a description and any photos or video you can provide. If a fault, transit damage, incorrect or missing item, or a mismatch with the confirmed design is verified, we will arrange a free remake, replacement or refund, taking the issue and your preference into account. We cover necessary return and replacement delivery costs.

The 7-day period is a suggested contact time to help resolve issues quickly. It does not remove or restrict your rights under applicable law. Please also contact us about problems discovered later.

Normal wear, chewing and misuse are not covered by unconditional free remakes, but we will help assess the cause. Customisation does not remove our responsibility for product quality.

3. How to request help

  1. Email tsunlab888@gmail.com with your order number and request, or call +852 9518 9171.
  2. We will check the order and issue, then agree the remake, replacement or refund with you.
  3. If a return is needed, we will provide the return address and sending instructions. Contact us first; do not send items to another address shown on the website or payment records.

Do not send full card numbers, CVC codes, security codes or identity-document photos. An order number and order email address are normally enough for an order enquiry.

4. Refund method & timing

Once a refund is confirmed, we will initiate it through the original payment method and notify your order email address. Processing times depend on your bank or payment provider. Contact us if a refund is taking longer than expected.

If you see duplicate charges, contact us so we can check them. Any confirmed duplicate payment will be refunded. We do not require you to accept a gift voucher instead of a cash refund that is due.

5. Your consumer rights

This policy does not exclude or restrict refund, replacement, product-quality or other consumer rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law. Later policy changes do not retrospectively reduce rights for orders already placed.

尚未開始製作，可申請取消並全額退款。定製開始後不接受單純改變主意的退換；品質問題、運輸損壞、錯貨或與確認內容不符，仍可申請免費補做、更換或退款。

1. 取消訂單及修改定製內容

需要取消或修改時，請盡快電郵聯絡我們，註明訂單號。我們會核查是否已開始製作。未開始製作：取消訂單可獲全額退款。已開始製作：通常無法接受無理由取消或修改；如仍可調整，我們會先說明可行方案及費用，取得你同意後處理。

付款前請核對狗狗名字、頭像款式、顏色、配飾、主人電話及姓名。若我們未按你確認的內容製作，會免費更正；若你提交的資料有誤，請及時聯絡，我們會按製作進度協助處理。

2. 品質問題、損壞、錯貨或缺件

請盡量在收貨後 7 日內聯絡我們，並提供訂單號、問題說明及方便提供的照片或影片。經核實屬於品質問題、運輸損壞、錯貨、缺件或與確認內容不符，我們會結合問題及你的意願安排免費補做、更換或退款，必要的退回及重寄費用由我們承擔。

7 日是便於盡快處理問題的建議聯絡期限，並不取消或限制你在適用法律下的權利；較後才發現的問題，也請聯絡我們。

日常磨損、寵物啃咬、不當使用等不屬於無條件免費補做範圍，但我們仍會協助判斷原因，不會以「定製商品」為由拒絕應承擔的品質責任。

3. 如何申請退換或退款？

  1. 電郵至 tsunlab888@gmail.com，寫明訂單號和訴求；也可致電 +852 9518 9171。
  2. 我們核對訂單及問題，與你確認補做、更換或退款安排。
  3. 需要退回商品時，我們會提供退回地址及寄件方式。請先聯絡，不要自行寄到網站或付款資料中的其他地址。

請勿發送完整銀行卡號、CVC、安全驗證碼或身份證照片。一般訂單查詢只需訂單號及下單電郵。

4. 退款方式與到賬

確認退款後，我們會透過原付款渠道發起退款，並以訂單電郵通知你。銀行或支付機構的處理時間不同，實際到賬時間以其安排為準；如遲遲未到賬，請聯絡我們查詢退款狀態。

若發生重複扣款，請先聯絡核對；確認重複收取的款項將退回。不會強制以禮品券代替應退現金。

5. 消費者權利

本政策不會排除或限制適用法律下不能排除的退款、退換、商品品質或其他消費者權利。政策更新不追溯削減已提交訂單的權益。
