4. NFC features & care

NFC opens a pet-profile link on compatible devices. An internet connection is needed to view the online page. The tag is not a GPS tracker, does not provide real-time location tracking and cannot guarantee the recovery of a lost pet. It does not replace a lead, supervision or other safety measures.

Regularly check the tag and attachments are secure. Avoid chewing or swallowing of small parts, and stop using damaged items. Protect the tag from high heat, strong impacts and improper use.

4. NFC 功能及使用注意

NFC 用於在兼容裝置上開啟寵物檔案連結，瀏覽線上網頁需要網絡連接。它並非 GPS 定位器，不提供即時位置追蹤，也不能保證找回走失寵物，不替代牽引、看護或其他安全措施。

請定期檢查狗牌及連接配件是否牢固，避免寵物啃咬或吞食小零件；出現破損時停止使用。請妥善保護標籤，避免高溫、猛烈撞擊及不當使用。
