Shipping policy

Made for your pet. Delivered with care.

Updated 9 October 2026 · Applies to orders placed after this policy is published

Standard Hong Kong delivery is included.
Production: approximately 1–3 days after payment and all required details are confirmed. Delivery: approximately 1–3 days after dispatch. These are separate stages.

1. Delivery areas & charges

We currently accept orders for delivery within Hong Kong by courier or standard parcel service. Standard delivery costs are included. Orders for other regions are not yet available directly; please contact us before paying.

If an address cannot be served, we will contact you to agree on a suitable address. If no delivery arrangement can be made, the order can be cancelled and the payment refunded. We will not add delivery charges without your agreement.

2. When production begins

  1. Complete your tag design and pay through Stripe.
  2. After payment, return to the website and submit the recipient’s name, phone number, full address and pet profile.
  3. Production time begins when payment and all details needed for production are confirmed.

If required details are missing, the production period does not start on the payment date. If you cannot find the details form after paying, keep your order number and confirmation email and contact us. Please do not pay again.

3. Production & delivery times

  • Production: approximately 1–3 days after payment and required details are confirmed.
  • Delivery: approximately 1–3 days after dispatch.

These are estimates, not guaranteed arrival dates. Changes to your details, severe weather or carrier delays may affect timing. If we expect a delay, we will notify you of the revised schedule. If it does not work for you, contact us to discuss the options, including cancellation and a refund where applicable.

After dispatch, we will email the carrier details and available tracking information to your order email address. Please also check your spam folder.

4. Address changes, missing or damaged parcels

Contact us as soon as possible before dispatch to change your address. Once dispatched, we can ask the carrier for help but cannot guarantee interception or an address change. Any extra redelivery costs caused by an incorrect address you supplied will be explained and agreed with you first. We cover costs caused by our own addressing error.

If your parcel is overdue, tracking is unusual, or the item arrives damaged or incorrect, send your order number and a description. Include photos of the item and packaging if convenient. We will help investigate and arrange redelivery, a remake or a refund under our returns & cancellation policy; you will not be asked to pursue the carrier on your own.

配送政策

從專屬設計，到送到你和毛孩子身邊。

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 9 日 · 適用於本政策上線後提交的訂單

配送政策退款與取消私隱政策服務條款聯絡我們
售價已包含香港標準配送。
付款及所需資料確認後，預計 1–3 天製作；寄出後，預計 1–3 天送達。製作與配送時間須分開計算。

1. 配送範圍與費用

目前接受香港地區的訂單，採用快遞或標準包裹配送，標準配送不另收費。其他地區暫不開放直接下單，請先聯絡客服確認，勿直接付款。

如地址無法配送，我們會先聯絡你協商可配送地址；無法達成配送安排時，可取消訂單並退還已付款項。未經你確認，不會追加配送費用。

2. 甚麼時候開始製作？

  1. 完成狗牌定製並透過 Stripe 付款。
  2. 付款成功後，返回網站填寫並提交收貨人、電話、詳細地址及寵物檔案。
  3. 付款和製作所需資料均已確認後，開始計算製作時間。

尚未提交完整資料時，製作時間不會從付款當天開始計算。若付款後未看到資料填寫頁，請保留訂單號和付款確認電郵，並聯絡我們，不要重複付款。

3. 製作及配送時間

  • 製作：付款及所需資料確認後，預計 1–3 天。
  • 配送：寄出後預計 1–3 天。

以上為預計時間，並非保證送達日期。資料修改、惡劣天氣或承運商延誤可能影響時效。若預計無法按原安排完成，我們會通知你新的時間；如無法接受，可聯絡我們協商，包括在適用情況下取消及退款。

寄出後，我們會透過訂單電郵提供承運商及可用的追蹤資訊。請同時檢查垃圾郵件資料夾。

4. 更改地址、未收到或包裹損壞

請在寄出前盡快聯絡我們更改地址。包裹寄出後，我們會協助向承運商查詢，但不能保證攔截或改址成功。因客人提供錯誤地址導致的額外重寄費用，會先說明並取得同意；因我們寄錯地址產生的費用由我們承擔。

超過預計時效仍未收到、追蹤顯示異常，或收到損壞或錯誤商品，請提供訂單號及情況說明；方便時附上包裝和商品照片。我們會協助調查，並按退款與取消政策安排補寄、補做或退款，不要求你獨自向承運商追討。
