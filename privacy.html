Privacy policy

Know what stays with your order, and what appears on a public pet profile.

Updated 9 October 2026

ShippingReturns & cancellationsPrivacy policyTerms of serviceContact us
An NFC pet profile is a public webpage, not a private album. Anyone who scans the tag or obtains the profile link may view, copy or share its public details. Do not add a full home address, identity documents or payment details to the pet profile. The name and phone number printed on the tag are also visible to others.

1. Who handles your information?

This policy applies to the TSUN88 shop and its NFC pet profile service. For personal-data enquiries, contact the customer-service team responsible for the shop’s data handling at tsunlab888@gmail.com or +852 9518 9171.

2. What we collect & why

  • Order and customisation details: your order number, email, design, names, photos and supplied contact details, to create previews, make the product, verify payment, send order updates and provide after-sales support.
  • Delivery details: the recipient’s name, phone and full address, for shipping, carrier contact and delivery issues. These fields are not automatically added to the public NFC profile.
  • Pet profile details: the photo, breed, birthday, sex, identifying features, owner and backup contacts, general area, notes, diet or allergies, personality and vet contacts you provide, to create a publicly accessible pet ID page.
  • Payment status: the order amount, currency, Stripe payment reference and status, to verify transactions, handle refunds and reconcile payments. Card details are handled on Stripe’s payment page; our customiser does not collect full card numbers or CVC codes.
  • Technical and communication records: access and error logs needed to run the service, customer-service emails and order-notification records, for maintenance, security and enquiries.

Fields marked required are needed for the relevant step. Without a necessary email, contact number or delivery details, we may be unable to complete payment, production or shipping. Optional pet-profile fields can be left blank. Before providing someone else’s contact details or photos, make sure you are entitled to do so and have informed them of the public use.

3. What becomes public?

Information submitted for the NFC profile, including owner and backup phone numbers, may appear on the public page. Only provide contact details you are comfortable making public. Use a general area for the location, not a door number or full home address.

Your order email and delivery address are not automatically published because you entered them on an order or delivery form. If you copy them into a public profile field such as a note, they will also be public. Public pages may be saved by others or indexed by search engines; we cannot guarantee the removal of copies already made by third parties.

4. Who receives information?

Where necessary for the purposes above, we share relevant information with:

  • People handling our orders, production and after-sales support, and delivery carriers.
  • Stripe: payments, refunds, transaction verification and payment-risk management.
  • Supabase: storage and delivery of NFC pet profiles.
  • Vercel: website hosting and order interfaces.
  • Google / Gmail: sending order and production notifications and receiving support emails. Order notifications may include design images, delivery details and profile links.
  • Regulators or law-enforcement bodies entitled to receive the information when required by law.

Service providers may process information outside Hong Kong under their applicable terms. Visitors to public profiles are not our contracted service providers. We do not sell order information to third parties for their independent marketing.

5. Browser storage

The website uses browser localStorage to save orders and related details so the payment and information-entry process can be resumed in the same browser. This may include designs, contact and delivery details, payment references and profile information. Your language preference is also saved in this browser.

Other people using a shared device may be able to access these local records. Prefer a personal device. Clearing website data may prevent unfinished orders from being restored automatically; save your order number and confirmation email first. Clearing local data does not automatically delete records held by us, payment providers or cloud services. Third-party pages such as Stripe may use their own cookies or storage, as described in their privacy notices.

6. Retention & protection

We retain information as needed for production, delivery, support, reconciliation and applicable legal obligations, and no longer than needed for those purposes. Public pet profiles are retained to provide the ongoing NFC service. Contact us if you want a profile taken out of public view. Records relating to unfinished transactions, disputes or legal retention duties may still need to be kept.

The website transmits information over HTTPS, and card payments are handled by Stripe. Internal access is limited according to business needs, but internet transmission and public sharing cannot be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Access, corrections, deletion & unpublishing

Email tsunlab888@gmail.com to request access to or correction of personal data, request deletion, or ask for a pet profile to be taken out of public view. Include your order number, the relevant profile link and what you would like changed. We will reasonably verify your identity and explain the outcome or any legal reason why a record cannot be deleted.

Closing a page or clearing browser history does not unpublish an NFC profile. Contact us about an existing profile; if we take it offline, the NFC link may no longer display that profile. Do not send identity-document photos, card numbers or verification codes in your initial email.

8. Policy updates

The latest update date appears on this page. If the way we use information changes materially, we will update this notice and provide notice or obtain consent where required.

私隱政策

分清訂單資料和公開檔案，放心決定填寫甚麼。

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 9 日

配送政策退款與取消私隱政策服務條款聯絡我們
NFC 寵物檔案是公開網頁，並非私人相簿。任何掃描狗牌 NFC 或取得檔案連結的人，都可能查看、複製或分享你提交的公開資料。不要在寵物檔案填寫詳細住址、身份證明文件或付款資料。狗牌背面的姓名及電話也會公開可見。

1. 誰處理資料、如何聯絡？

本政策適用於 TSUN88 商店及其 NFC 寵物檔案服務。涉及個人資料的問題，請聯絡負責本店資料處理的客服：tsunlab888@gmail.com；電話 +852 9518 9171。

2. 收集甚麼、為何需要？

  • 訂單與定製資料：訂單號、電郵、狗牌設計、名字、照片及你填寫的聯絡方式，用於生成預覽、製作商品、核對付款、通知訂單及售後。
  • 配送資料：收貨人、聯絡電話和詳細地址，用於寄送、物流聯絡和處理配送問題。這些欄位不會自動加入公開 NFC 檔案。
  • 寵物檔案：你填寫的寵物照片、品種、生日、性別、特徵、主人及備用聯絡人、所在地區、留言、飲食或過敏、性格及獸醫聯絡方式等，用於生成可公開存取的寵物身份網頁。
  • 付款狀態：訂單金額、幣種、Stripe 付款編號及付款狀態，用於核驗交易、退款和對賬。銀行卡資料在 Stripe 付款頁處理，本站定製頁面不收集完整卡號或 CVC。
  • 技術及通訊紀錄：服務運作所需的存取或錯誤紀錄、客服電郵及訂單通知紀錄，用於維護、安全及處理查詢。

表格中標註必填的資料用於完成相應步驟；不提供必要的電郵、聯絡電話或收貨資料，可能無法完成付款、製作或寄送。選填的寵物檔案資料可留空。提供他人聯絡方式或照片前，請確保有權提供並已告知對方公開用途。

3. 哪些資料會公開？

提交用於 NFC 檔案的資料，包括主人及備用聯絡人電話，可能顯示在公開網頁。請只提供願意公開的聯絡方式；「所在地區」建議填寫大致區域，不填門牌或完整住址。

訂單電郵及快遞地址不會因填寫在訂單或配送表格而自動公開；如果你把相同內容手動寫入檔案留言等公開欄位，它們也會被公開。公開網頁可能被他人保存或搜尋引擎收錄，我們無法保證收回第三方已複製的內容。

4. 會向誰提供資料？

為完成上述用途，我們會在必要範圍內向以下對象提供相關資料：

  • 本店處理訂單、製作和售後的人員，以及負責配送的承運商。
  • Stripe：處理付款、退款、交易核驗及支付風險管理。
  • Supabase：儲存及提供 NFC 寵物檔案。
  • Vercel：託管網站及運行訂單介面。
  • Google / Gmail：發送訂單和生產通知、接收客服電郵；訂單通知可能包含設計圖、收貨資料和檔案連結。
  • 法律要求時，有權取得資料的監管或執法機構。

相關服務商可能在香港以外處理資料，並按其適用條款處理服務所需的數據。公開檔案的訪客不屬於我們的受託服務商。我們不會把訂單資料出售給第三方用作其獨立推廣。

5. 瀏覽器本機儲存

本網站使用瀏覽器 localStorage 保存訂單及相關資料，以便在同一瀏覽器中恢復付款和資料填寫流程；內容可能包括設計、聯絡方式、收貨資料、付款編號及檔案資訊。你的語言選擇也會儲存在此瀏覽器。

共用裝置上的其他使用者可能存取這些本機紀錄。請優先使用個人裝置。清除網站資料可能使未完成訂單無法自動恢復；清除前請保存訂單號及確認電郵。清除本機資料不會自動刪除我們、支付機構或雲端保存的紀錄。Stripe 等第三方網頁還可能使用其自身的 Cookie 或儲存方式，詳見其私隱說明。

6. 保存時間與保護措施

我們按製作、配送、售後、對賬和適用法定義務所需保存資料，不應超出相關目的所需期限。公開寵物檔案為持續提供 NFC 服務而保留；如希望停止公開，可聯絡我們提出申請。涉及未完成交易、爭議或法定保存義務的紀錄，可能仍需保留。

網站透過 HTTPS 傳輸資料，銀行卡付款由 Stripe 處理。我們按業務需要限制內部使用，但互聯網傳輸及公開分享無法保證絕對安全。

7. 查詢、更正、刪除及停止公開

你可電郵至 tsunlab888@gmail.com，申請查閱或更正個人資料、提出刪除請求，或要求停止公開寵物檔案。請提供訂單號及相關檔案連結，說明希望處理的內容。我們會進行合理身份核對，並告知處理結果或依法不能刪除的原因。

關閉網頁或刪除瀏覽器紀錄不會停止 NFC 檔案公開。需要處理已生成的檔案時，請聯絡客服；如我們下線原檔案，NFC 連結可能不再顯示該檔案。不要在初次電郵中發送身份證照片、銀行卡號或驗證碼。

8. 政策更新

本頁註明最近更新日期。若資料用途發生重要變化，我們會更新說明，並在適用要求下提供通知或取得所需同意。
